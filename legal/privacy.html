Privacy Policy

ASP NYC – Alternate Side Parking (Android app com.cymbit.paras). Effective October 20, 2026.

ASP NYC is a free app by Cymbit that shows whether New York City alternate side parking rules and parking meters are in effect, and can send you a notification about it. This page explains what the app collects, why, and how to have it removed. The app shows no ads and never sells or shares your data with advertisers.

What the app collects

Data Why Where it is stored
An anonymous user ID Created automatically by Firebase Authentication the first time you open the app, so your settings can be saved without an account, email or password. Firebase (Google), United States
Notification settings The hours you choose for the current-day and next-day notifications, and whether you want exceptions-only or weekend notifications. Cloud Firestore (Google), under your anonymous ID
A push notification token A device token from Firebase Cloud Messaging so the app can deliver the notifications you turned on. It is only stored if you grant the notification permission. Cloud Firestore (Google), under your anonymous ID
App version and sign-in times The app version you installed and the dates you first opened and last opened the app, saved alongside your settings so stale records can be recognised. Cloud Firestore (Google), under your anonymous ID
App usage and performance data Which screens are opened, app version, device model and OS version, crash and load-time diagnostics, via Firebase Analytics and Firebase Performance Monitoring. Both create a random app-instance identifier on your device to group these events; it is not linked to your name or account. Used only to find problems and see which features are used. Firebase (Google)
Donation purchase If you choose to support development with the in-app donation, Google Play processes the payment. The app never sees your payment details and stores no purchase history of its own. Google Play

Dark-mode preference and a copy of the settings above are also kept on your device only, so the app works offline. The parking schedule itself comes from public NYC data and contains nothing about you.

What the app does not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number, or password. There is no sign-up.
  • No location. The app does not ask for or use your position.
  • No contacts, photos, files, or calendar access.
  • No advertising identifier, and no ad networks.

Sharing

Data is processed by Google on our behalf through Firebase and Google Play, under Google's privacy and security terms. We do not share, sell or rent data to anyone else, and we do not use it for advertising.

Retention and deletion

  • Your settings and token stay stored while you use the app. A token is removed automatically when a notification cannot be delivered because the token is no longer valid (for example after you uninstall).
  • Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on the device. The server-side record stays until you ask for it to be deleted.
  • To have your server-side record (anonymous ID, settings, token) deleted, open Settings → About this App in the app, copy the anonymous ID shown there, and email it to cymbit@gmail.com. The ID is the only way to find your record, because there is no account, email or name attached to it. Requests are completed within 30 days.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Security

All data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored in Google's Firebase infrastructure.
